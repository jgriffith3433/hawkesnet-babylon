# Hawkes Net for Babylon: world bible

The demo arena that ships with `@hawkesgen/net-babylon`: a small multiplayer place on Hawkes Net’s free `netdemo` rooms, meant to show the package (shared room, avatars, eased move, touch stick) rather than a full game.

## Areas

**Flagstone arena** — dusk open court, cool fog, warm torch light. About 36×36 units of flagstone floor with a painted green ring at the centre. Enter by opening the demo page (joins area `babylon-demo`); leave by closing the tab. On a phone the same place fills the screen with a one-line status and a bottom-left joystick.

## Landmarks

The **glowing ring** on the floor at the centre — visible from the edge pillars and the first thing a second player steers toward.

## Who lives here

**Visitors** (each tab is a named capsule, “Player NNN”). They walk the floor, wave (E), and leave with a fade. No NPCs; life is other people who open the page.

## What happens

- Walk with WASD or the touch joystick; footsteps while moving, silence when still.
- Wave (E); a chime when someone joins, a short farewell when they leave.
- A shared crate at the ring spins when you host it.
- Ambient: dusk sky, torch flicker on the stone, soft fog.
- Phone: Sound sits bottom-right, clear of the stick; the info line expands on tap.

## Story beats

1. You appear on the ring as a capsule with your name.
2. Another visitor’s capsule pops in with a chime; you see each other move.
3. A wave passes both ways; when they leave, their avatar fades out.
